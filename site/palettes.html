How the limits are kept

  • 8-bit sprites

    Creatures, heroes and items: one outline, one body colour and one light, so three colours and transparent. NES and C64 entries are real palette entries; Game Boy uses its four greens. Islands are backgrounds, not sprites: four to seven colours on NES and C64.

  • 16-bit colour depth

    SNES colours are rounded to 5 bits per channel and Mega Drive colours to 3 bits, the depth of those machines' colour registers. PICO-8 snaps to its fixed 16.

  • Not claimed

    Sprite size, scanline and tile limits of the real consoles are not enforced. The palettes are exact; the hardware around them is not simulated.
