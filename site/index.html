Why it looks right

  • Real limits

    8-bit creatures, heroes and items are three colours and transparent, the NES rule (Game Boy: its four greens). 16-bit sprites sit inside the 32,768-colour SNES and 512-colour Mega Drive colour sets and use at most ten colours, under the limit of 15. Colour is exact; sprite sizes, scanlines and tiles are not simulated.

  • Hand-made habits

    Dark outline, rim light top left, shade bottom right, and shadows that drift cool while lights drift warm. Islands dither between bands on 16-bit and band flat on 8-bit.

  • Seeded

    A seed, a kind, a depth and a palette name one sprite for good. The browser and the command line draw identical pixels.
